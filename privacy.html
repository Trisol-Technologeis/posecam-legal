Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PoseCam: Photo Pose Guide ("PoseCam", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app on iPhone. PoseCam is published by Rehman Ullah, an individual developer based in Pakistan, who is responsible for your information as the data controller.

Questions or requests: rehman.ullah.1179@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • The camera and pose tracking run entirely on your iPhone. Camera frames and photos are never uploaded to us.
  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We collect anonymous usage and crash data to improve the app, and subscription status to provide Premium.
  • We don't show ads, sell your information, use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

2. Information processed on your device only

  • Camera. With your permission, the live camera image is analysed on your iPhone by Google ML Kit's on-device pose detection to place body landmarks, score your pose and trigger the auto-shutter. Frames are processed in memory and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos. Photos you take are saved to your Photos library (in a "PoseCam" album) only when you choose to keep them, and shared only through the iOS share sheet when you choose. We need photo library permission solely to save them there; the app does not read the rest of your library.
  • Preferences. Favourite poses, recently viewed poses, language, theme and camera settings are stored on your device.

3. Information we collect

Usage and diagnostic data

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): app usage events such as screens opened, poses viewed, photos captured and paywall interactions; a random app-instance identifier; app version, device model, iOS version, language and approximate country derived from the IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports and related diagnostics (stack traces, device state, a random installation identifier).
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): an installation identifier used to deliver app configuration, such as which server provides the pose catalog.

Subscription data

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details. To check and restore Premium we use RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app user ID created on your device, your App Store transaction information (product, purchase, trial and expiry dates, price, currency, storefront country) and basic device information (app and iOS version, device model, IP address).

Pose catalog requests

The app downloads the pose catalog and its images from our server and from Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3). Like any web request, these reveal your IP address and basic request details to the servers, which may keep short-term technical logs. No personal information is sent with these requests.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features and Premium, including restoring purchases.
  • To fix crashes, measure performance and understand which features are used, so we can improve the app.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (where GDPR applies): performance of our contract with you (providing the app and Premium) and our legitimate interests (keeping the app working and improving it).

6. Retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted by Google. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide and account for the subscription. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off camera or photo access at any time in iPhone Settings → PoseCam.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device and resets its identifiers.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your information. Because there are no accounts, we can't link analytics data to you by name; email us with your request and we'll help as far as we can.

8. Children

PoseCam is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. They use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.
