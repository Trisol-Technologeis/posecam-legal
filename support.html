Subscriptions

What does PoseCam Premium include?

Browsing poses, the camera, live pose guides and coaching are free to try. Premium lets you keep the photos you take: review, save to your PoseCam album and share them, including auto-shutter shots.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After the trial, the yearly price shown in the app is charged to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → PoseCam → Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from PoseCam: Settings → Manage subscription. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone

Open PoseCam → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Camera & photos

The camera is black or says access is denied

Open iPhone Settings → PoseCam → turn on Camera. Pose tracking runs on your iPhone; camera frames are never uploaded.

Where are my photos saved?

In your Photos library, in an album called PoseCam. If saving fails, check iPhone Settings → PoseCam → Photos is set to Full Access.

The live score doesn't move

Live matching needs your whole body (or the part the pose uses) in frame with good light. Step back, and keep the phone steady at chest height.
